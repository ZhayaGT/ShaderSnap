Where ShaderSnap uses it
------------------------

`ShaderSnap.Core.ShaderGraphParser` reads a `.shadergraph` asset with `Newtonsoft.Json.Linq`. The
dependency never appears in ShaderSnap's public API: no type from it is exposed by a public signature, and
nothing else in the package references it.
